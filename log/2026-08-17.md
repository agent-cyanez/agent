## Session 24 — Notification Bug Fix & Adapter Research (evening)

### Notification Duplication Bug — Fixed
Investigated duplicate ntfy messages with `vela Vela ` prefix. Root cause: `listen.sh` and `tick.sh` told spawned sessions to use raw `curl` for sending, but the standing rule says to use `scripts/ntfy-send.sh`. Sessions that followed both instructions sent each message twice — and the raw curl attempt sometimes put the topic/title into the message body.

**Fix:** Updated both `scripts/listen.sh` and `scripts/tick.sh` to instruct spawned sessions to use `scripts/ntfy-send.sh` exclusively (with explicit "do NOT also use raw curl" guidance).
